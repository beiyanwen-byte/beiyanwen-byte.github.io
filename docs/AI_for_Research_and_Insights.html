1.1 AI for research and insights

English 🇺🇸中文 🇨🇳
Sometimes, making a big decision at work can feel like you're answering the final question on a game show. Everyone's watching, and time is running out. Are you gonna win the big prize, or are you gonna go home empty-handed? It's easy to start doubting yourself.有时候，在工作中做出重大决定，就像是在答题节目里回答最后一道题。所有人都在看着，时间正在流逝。你是会赢得大奖，还是空手而归？很容易就开始怀疑自己。
Now, you'd probably feel more confident making a decision if you just had more information. Usually that means doing in-depth research and pulling together things like competitor profiles and consumer trends and industry insights. But creating just one of those reports could take days, or weeks or even months.如果你有更多的信息，你做出决定时可能会更有信心。通常这意味着要做深入的研究，整合竞争对手分析、消费者趋势和行业洞察等内容。但仅仅制作一份这样的报告就可能需要几天、几周甚至几个月的时间。
Now, this is where AI can be an incredibly valuable research partner. It does way more than just produce a report faster. It can scour a range of sources, get to the key takeaways, and help you deliver a stronger report. It's a whole new way of conducting research and building understanding on a topic.这就是AI可以成为极其有价值的研究伙伴的地方。它不仅仅是更快地生成报告。它可以搜索各种来源，提取关键要点，并帮助你交付更有力的报告。这是一种全新的研究方式和对主题建立理解的方法。
Now, let's be clear, this doesn't mean that the fundamentals of good research are going away. You still need to verify all of your information, and you still need to build your own arguments. And when you're faced with really difficult or ambiguous challenges, the ones where there's really no simple solution, you still need to consider all the angles and make the final decision.现在，让我们明确一点，这并不意味着良好研究的基础正在消失。你仍然需要验证所有信息，仍然需要建立自己的论点。当你面临真正困难或模糊的挑战——那些没有简单解决方案的情况——你仍然需要考虑所有角度并做出最终决定。
In this course, you'll learn how you can use AI to build deeper understanding in almost any area, and you'll discover how to transform uncertainty into solid, confident recommendations.在本课程中，你将学习如何使用AI在几乎任何领域建立更深入的理解，你将发现如何将不确定性转化为扎实、自信的建议。
First, we'll show you how to research a complex topic with Gemini Deep Research. It can find and analyze multiple sources on your behalf, synthesize that information, and reason through its findings.首先，我们将向你展示如何使用Gemini Deep Research研究复杂主题。它可以代表你查找和分析多个来源，综合这些信息，并通过其发现进行推理。
Then, you'll gain the ability to cut directly to key takeaways from any materials that come your way, even if the most important nuggets are scattered across multiple documents.然后，你将获得直接从任何材料中提取关键要点的能力，即使最重要的信息分散在多个文档中。
You'll learn how to manage that information, reports, articles, websites, business documents, meeting notes, almost anything, and generate useful summaries in text, audio, and video formats using Gemini Notebook.你将学习如何管理这些信息——报告、文章、网站、商业文档、会议记录，几乎所有内容——并使用Gemini Notebook生成文本、音频和视频格式的有用摘要。
After completing this course, you'll be able to consistently turn complexity into clarity. You'll be well on your way to being the person that colleagues rely on to tackle tough, ambiguous problems.完成本课程后，你将能够持续地将复杂性转化为清晰性。你将成为同事依赖你来解决棘手、模糊问题的人。
And with the feedback from Gemini, you'll always feel like you have support in making huge decisions. It's a shift that gives you the power to enhance your understanding on almost any topic. So, if getting a new way to learn quickly sounds good to you, then let's get started.借助Gemini的反馈，你将始终感觉在做出重大决策时有支持。这种转变赋予你增强对几乎任何主题理解的能力。所以，如果一种新的快速学习方式听起来不错，那么让我们开始吧。

You are onboarding a new designer. You provide a project brief doc, a design principles PDF, and a video walkthrough of the brand's style to the designer. You want to help them quickly understand the key points from all sources. From the options below, what is the most effective way to use NotebookLM to help the designer synthesize this information?

选项

选项内容结果
AUpload all three sources into a single Notebook and prompt the tool to generate a one-page summary that synthesizes the key design principles and project goals.✅ 正确
BGenerate an Audio Overview that synthesizes each of the three sources, then send it to the designer to listen to in sequential order.❌
CPrompt Gemini Notebook to search the internet for your company's brand guidelines, then create a summary that includes the most up-to-date design trends.❌
DUpload the text documents into a single Notebook and prompt the tool to synthesize the key design information into a comprehensive summary that includes a link to the video.❌

答案解析

正确答案是A。NotebookLM可以上传多种格式的文档（PDF、文本、视频），然后生成综合摘要。将所有来源上传到单个笔记本并生成摘要，是最有效的方式。

Then you pasted the list into a new chat, instructing Gemini to act as the expert persona and review your menu ideas. Based on the process for designing an expert persona, what is the most effective next step to improve the generated feedback?

题目

You are an event planner organizing a company-wide hybrid training session. You have a strict constraint: you must keep both the in-person and remote attendees fully engaged for four straight hours without the ability to use virtual breakout rooms. You want to use a custom AI expert to pressure-test your agenda. What is the most effective prompt to evaluate your agenda against this constraint?

选项

选项内容结果
A"Review my proposed four-hour agenda and identify potential engagement drops from the perspective of an expert corporate trainer, considering the constraint that this is a hybrid event with no virtual breakout rooms."✅ 正确
B"Create a four-hour training schedule with 15-minute breaks built in for both in-person and remote attendees, considering the constraint that this is a hybrid even with no virtual breakout rooms."❌
C"Evaluate my proposed four-hour agenda from an expert trainer's perspective and provide a general list of its overall strengths and weaknesses."❌
D"Provide a list of expert tips on how to maintain engagement during a four-hour hybrid meeting."❌

答案解析

正确答案是A。这个提示有效地将AI设定为专家角色（企业培训师），并明确说明了约束条件（混合活动、无虚拟分组讨论室），要求识别潜在的参与度下降点。

题目

You are a custom cabinet maker. You are using Gemini Notebook to summarize various supplier catalogs, pricing sheets, and client emails for an upcoming kitchen remodel. You notice that the generated summary claims a specific, high-end wood stain is currently discounted by your main supplier, but you recall them discontinuing that product last year. Based on best practices for checking AI-generated output, what is the most efficient next step?

选项

选项内容结果
AUpload the catalog to Gemini Notebook then ask for a list of discontinued items to check if the wood stain is included.❌
BReview your past notes to find the supplier's pricing sheets.❌
CFollow the citation link in the generated summary directly to the specific supplier catalog to verify the item's status.✅ 正确
DPrompt Gemini Notebook to generate a new summary to see if the model consistently mentions the discount on the wood stain.❌

答案解析

正确答案是C。Gemini Notebook在生成摘要时会包含引用链接。最有效的方法是直接点击引用链接，验证原始文档中的信息是否准确。

题目

You are an event coordinator. You are using a custom AI expert to evaluate proposals for sourcing decorative materials. Your objective is to ensure the materials fit a "Winter Wonderland" theme and stay under a strict $500 budget. You notice the generated feedback ignores the budget and the theme. Based on best practices for designing custom AI experts, which revised prompt will most effectively fix this issue for future use?

选项

选项内容结果
A"Search the internet for cheap winter decorations and automatically replace the items in our proposals with the most affordable options."❌
B"Review our decorative material proposals and generate 50 more ideas for winter wonderland decorations."❌

D"Think step-by-step about general event styling principles before you read our decorative material proposals."❌

正确答案是C。这个提示明确说明了预算限制（$500）和主题要求（Winter Wonderland），并要求标记不符合要求的项目，格式化为分类列表。这将帮助AI更好地理解你的需求并提供相关的反馈。
