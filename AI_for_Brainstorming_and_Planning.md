| First, you'll learn techniques to generate ideas with you. We'll simulate experts and push creative boundaries to uncover solutions you never would've considered. | 首先，你将学习与你一起产生想法的技术。我们将模拟专家并推动创意边界，以发现你从未考虑过的解决方案。 |
| Next, you'll pressure test solutions. Now, the best ideas are ones that can stand up to potential problems in the future. | 接下来，你将对解决方案进行压力测试。最好的想法是那些能够应对未来潜在问题的想法。 |
| You'll learn to use Gemini as a devil's advocate to find risks in your strategy before they become problems. | 你将学习使用Gemini作为魔鬼代言人，在你的策略变成问题之前发现风险。 |
| Finally, you'll manage your project by getting everyone on the same page.
