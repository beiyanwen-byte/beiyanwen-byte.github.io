### 2.2 Test your app to identify and fix bugs

> 📚 **阅读时长**：4分钟

| English 🇺🇸 | 中文 🇨🇳 |
|-------------|----------|
| Debugging, or the process of fixing what's broken, works like comparing what you built with what the manual shows, backing up a step, and fixing one piece. | 调试，也就是修复故障的过程，就像将已完成的内容与说明进行比较，退回一步，然后修复其中一个部分。 |
| When something looks off, describe the difference between what you wanted and what you got, and direct the model to make the fix. | 当出现异常时，描述预期结果与实际结果之间的差异，并指导模型进行修复。 |
| As you test, fix, and test again, you're continuously refining your prompts to improve the quality of the AI-generated output. This cycle is called iteration. | 在测试、修复、再次测试的过程中，你会不断完善提示，以提高AI生成结果的质量。这个循环称为迭代。 |
| Almost every app needs iteration, so as you debug your own app, you'll develop a troubleshooting toolkit you'll use on everything you build. | 几乎每个应用都需要迭代。调试自己的应用时，你会形成一套可用于所有构建项目的故障排查工具集。 |
| **The confidence to debug your app:** Interpret an error, find the bug, and locate what needs to change, without needing to understand the code. | **调试应用的信心：** 无需理解代码，也能解释错误、找到问题并确定需要修改的地方。 |
| **The skill to communicate clearly with the model:** Describe the difference between what you wanted and what the app actually did, and give the model the context it needs to correct it. | **与模型清晰沟通的技能：** 描述预期结果与应用实际行为的差异，并提供模型修正问题所需的背景信息。 |
| **The ability to set rules that stick:** Add a guardrail the app follows every time, not just once. | **设定持久规则的能力：** 添加应用每次都必须遵循的防护规则，而不是只执行一次。 |
| **The instinct to stress-test:** Feed your app messy or wrong input a real user might enter, to find failures before others do. | **压力测试意识：** 输入真实用户可能提供的杂乱或错误数据，在他人发现问题前找出故障。 |
| In the next video, Ammaar starts with a digital marketing problem: turning a creative idea into an actionable shot-by-shot storyboard. | 在下一个视频中，Ammaar从一个数字营销问题开始：将创意想法转化为可执行的逐镜头分镜脚本。 |
| His app generates a three-column storyboard containing visuals, audio and dialogue, and shot type and duration. | 他的应用会生成三列分镜脚本，分别包含视觉内容、音频和对白，以及镜头类型和时长。 |
| Testing may reveal errors such as quota limits, but some problems are hallucinations caused by insufficient training data or incorrect assumptions. | 测试可能会发现配额限制等错误，但有些问题是由训练数据不足或错误假设导致的幻觉。 |
| A CTA, or call to action, tells viewers what to do next, such as “Get started for free.” A rule can require every storyboard to end with one. | CTA，即行动号召，是告诉观众下一步该做什么的文字，例如“免费开始”。可以设置规则，要求每个分镜脚本都以CTA结尾。 |
| **Expert insight: ask the model to debug its code.** “When something breaks, I describe what I expected versus what actually happened, then pass the error message straight back to the model.” | **专家洞察：要求模型调试代码。**“出现问题时，我会描述预期结果与实际结果，然后直接把错误信息反馈给模型。” |
