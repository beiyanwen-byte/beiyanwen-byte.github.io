### 2.3 Build with AI: Tie-breaker app for better decision making

> 🧪 **实验室时长**：12分钟

### 🧪 实验室步骤对照

| 步骤 | English | 中文 |
|------|---------|------|
| Step 1 | **Define your app**. In AI Studio, sign in, navigate to “Build AI apps,” and paste: “I want an app called The Tiebreaker that helps me make decisions. I want to provide a decision that I need to make, and I'd love AI's help in showing me different pros and cons. This can be through a pros and cons list, a comparison table, or even a SWOT analysis.” Then click “Build.” | **定义应用。** 在AI Studio中登录，进入“Build AI apps”，粘贴以下提示：“我想要一个名为The Tiebreaker的应用来帮助我做决定。我想提供一个需要做出的决定，并希望AI帮助我展示不同的优缺点。可以使用优缺点列表、比较表，甚至SWOT分析。”然后点击“Build”。 |
| Step 2 | **Test your application**. Enter: “Should I offer my software for free with paid features, or charge a flat $20/month premium plan?” Select “Pros and Cons List,” click “Get Analysis,” and review the results. | **测试应用。** 输入：“我应该免费提供软件并通过付费功能盈利，还是收取每月20美元的固定高级套餐费用？”选择“Pros and Cons List”，点击“Get Analysis”，并查看结果。 |
| Step 3 | **Troubleshoot and Auto-fix errors**. If the app breaks, click “Auto fix.” The model will analyze the error and rewrite the code to fix the bug. | **排查并自动修复错误。** 如果应用出错，点击“Auto fix”。模型会分析错误并重写代码以修复问题。 |
| Step 4 | **Verify the fix**. Enter: “What should I eat tonight: Sushi or Chinese food?” Select “Comparison Table,” click “Get Analysis,” and ensure the results display correctly. | **验证修复结果。** 输入：“我今晚应该吃什么：寿司还是中餐？”选择“Comparison Table”，点击“Get Analysis”，并确认结果正确显示。 |
| Next steps | Reflect on other decision-making frameworks you use at work. When satisfied with the functional app, close the page and return to the main course. | **下一步：** 思考你在工作中使用的其他决策框架。对功能完整的应用满意后，关闭页面并返回主课程。 |
