2.3 Build with AI: Tie-breaker app for better decision making

🧪 实验室时长：12分钟

📎 实验室材料链接

材料名称链接
Google AI StudioAI Studio

🧪 实验室步骤对照

步骤English中文
Step 1Define your app. In AI Studio, sign in, navigate to “Build AI apps,” and paste: “I want an app called The Tiebreaker that helps me make decisions. I want to provide a decision that I need to make, and I'd love AI's help in showing me different pros and cons. This can be through a pros and cons list, a comparison table, or even a SWOT analysis.” Then click “Build.”定义应用。 在AI Studio中登录，进入“Build AI apps”，粘贴以下提示：“我想要一个名为The Tiebreaker的应用来帮助我做决定。我想提供一个需要做出的决定，并希望AI帮助我展示不同的优缺点。可以使用优缺点列表、比较表，甚至SWOT分析。”然后点击“Build”。
Step 2Test your application. Enter: “Should I offer my software for free with paid features, or charge a flat $20/month premium plan?” Select “Pros and Cons List,” click “Get Analysis,” and review the results.测试应用。 输入：“我应该免费提供软件并通过付费功能盈利，还是收取每月20美元的固定高级套餐费用？”选择“Pros and Cons List”，点击“Get Analysis”，并查看结果。
Step 3Troubleshoot and Auto-fix errors. If the app breaks, click “Auto fix.” The model will analyze the error and rewrite the code to fix the bug.排查并自动修复错误。 如果应用出错，点击“Auto fix”。模型会分析错误并重写代码以修复问题。
Step 4Verify the fix. Enter: “What should I eat tonight: Sushi or Chinese food?” Select “Comparison Table,” click “Get Analysis,” and ensure the results display correctly.验证修复结果。 输入：“我今晚应该吃什么：寿司还是中餐？”选择“Comparison Table”，点击“Get Analysis”，并确认结果正确显示。
Next stepsReflect on other decision-making frameworks you use at work. When satisfied with the functional app, close the page and return to the main course.下一步： 思考你在工作中使用的其他决策框架。对功能完整的应用满意后，关闭页面并返回主课程。

3.3 Ammaar, what's your pro tip for building apps?

🎬 视频时长：1:02
English 🇺🇸中文 🇨🇳
My big pro tip is always break it up. Break it up in terms of the different blocks of the app that you're trying to make.我的重要建议是始终把任务拆分开来。按照你要构建的应用中的不同模块来拆分任务。
It's always helpful to actually tell the model, "Here's the project we're making as a whole," so it understands the whole picture or the whole story of the thing you're about to make.告诉模型“这是我们要构建的完整项目”总是很有帮助，这样它就能理解即将构建的事物的整体情况或完整故事。
Then, you can actually say, "Cool, I have this app I'm going to make. Let's start with the first part." And then, when it does that first part, say, "Now, let's do the second part." And you can build it brick by brick.然后，你可以说：“好的，我要构建这个应用。让我们从第一部分开始。”完成第一部分后再说：“现在，让我们做第二部分。”这样就可以一砖一瓦地构建应用。
When you do that, it's helpful because it'll probably get it right because it's actually focusing on the very specific tasks you're giving it.这样做很有帮助，因为模型会专注于你提供的具体任务，因此更可能正确完成任务。
It's also easier to correct errors because if you ask it to make this whole big thing, you don't actually know where it messed up. But if you're doing brick by brick, you know exactly which one actually causes that fault.这样也更容易纠正错误。因为如果你要求模型一次完成一个很大的整体，你并不知道错误出在哪里；但如果逐步构建，就能准确知道是哪一步导致了问题。
Be a little patient with it and break it up into steps just like you would if you were to give any person a really complex task, and it'll go a long way.对它耐心一点，把任务拆分成多个步骤，就像给一个人分配复杂任务一样，这会带来很大帮助。
